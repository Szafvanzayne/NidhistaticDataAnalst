I am an analytics leader with 15+ years of experience helping organizations turn complex data into clear insights, reliable reporting, and practical business decisions. I hold a Master's degree in Business Analytics & Data Science from Bentley University in Waltham, Massachusetts, and have built much of my career working with organizations and cross-functional teams in the United States. My work sits at the intersection of business strategy, data engineering, digital analytics, and business intelligence — allowing me to translate business questions into scalable analytics solutions.

Over the years, I have led analytics initiatives across web, marketing, content, audience, and business performance reporting. I have built dashboards, defined KPI frameworks, automated manual reporting processes, supported data warehouse design, and worked closely with business, technology, and data engineering teams to improve how organizations collect, structure, and use data.

My strength lies in combining technical depth with business context. I bring strong technical proficiency across analytics, reporting, automation, and data quality processes, while also leading stakeholder conversations, shaping reporting strategy, and helping teams understand what the data is really saying.

I bring a practical, outcome-focused approach to analytics. Whether the need is a leadership dashboard, a data audit, a reporting automation workflow, a digital analytics framework, or senior-level analytics guidance, I focus on building solutions that are clear, reliable, and useful for decision-making.

At the core of my work is a simple goal: to help teams move from scattered data and manual reporting to trusted insights, stronger data foundations, and confident business decisions. If your team needs help bringing clarity, structure, and trust to your data and reporting, let's connect. I'd be happy to explore how my analytics expertise can support your goals.
